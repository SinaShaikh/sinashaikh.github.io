Hi, I'm Sina! I am a first year Ph.D. student at MIT studying Political Science and Statistics. I am advised by Ariel White and supported by the institute-wide Presidential Fellowship.

I am interested in studying how computational techniques can be applied to study elections and political communication. I'm also interested in building new datasets and making existing ones more usable. For more details on my past work, see Research.

Over the past few years, I:

  • Studied elections and built datasets at the MIT Elections Lab
  • Worked for the former UN High Commissioner for Human Rights
  • Reported on elections for NBC and made graphics for the 13th sexiest man alive
  • Interned at the Brookings Institution
  • Graduated Summa Cum Laude from Penn with a BSE in CS
  • Won chess, soccer, and mock trial tournaments.
  • Read, ate, and watched as much as possible

I do my best work with others and am always looking for new collaborators. In 2026 I will be presenting at SPSA, ESRA, and POLMETH and attending APSA. I live in Boston and you can reach me at sinas100 at mit.edu.
